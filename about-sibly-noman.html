About Sibly Noman

Sibly Noman is a mechanical engineering student at Savonia University of Applied Sciences in Kuopio, Finland. His academic background also includes previous mechanical engineering studies with an aeronautics focus at Universiti Teknologi Malaysia.

His technical interests include computational fluid dynamics (CFD), heat transfer, thermal management, HVAC systems, mechanical design, aerospace engineering, antenna development and CubeSat-related systems. His work combines engineering fundamentals with simulation, coding, research and technical communication.

Engineering interests and skills

  • Simulation and thermal engineering: OpenFOAM, CFD workflows, heat transfer, indoor airflow and thermal-comfort analysis.
  • Mechanical engineering: mechanical systems, CAD, manufacturing considerations and engineering design.
  • Aerospace and RF: aeronautics, satellite communications, CubeSat subsystems and antenna research.
  • Computing and data: Python, MATLAB, C, SQL/SQLite, data analysis and interactive technical tools.

Selected engineering projects

Indoor Air CFD: an engineering project exploring indoor airflow, temperature distribution, air age and thermal-comfort indicators through computational modelling and visualization.

Data Center Thermal Monitoring Simulator (ThermoDC): a project focused on data-centre thermal conditions, monitoring and energy-optimization concepts.

Aerospace and antenna research: ongoing technical interests and project work involving antenna systems, wireless communication and small-satellite applications.
