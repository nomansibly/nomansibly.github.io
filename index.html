Hydraulic Rotational Test Bench

A five-person Savonia RDI team developed a rotational hydraulic teaching rig, from 3D assembly to a fabricated and commissioned prototype with a geared drive, adjustable frame, safety shielding and Parker IQAN controls.

My contribution: Coordinated purchasing and suppliers, documented project progress, compiled the final report and participated in system commissioning and troubleshooting.

500–1,000RPM tested

Engineering methods & specifications

Initial proximity-sensor triggering at a 5 mm gap was unreliable; the team introduced adjustable brackets and confirmed reliable operation at 1.5 mm. Resonance around 900 RPM prompted frame gussets and base dampers. The report describes resolved issues during team testing.

Indoor Air Lab

A reproducible ventilation study combining Python finite-volume screening, an eight-hour thermal model and two executed 3-D buoyant OpenFOAM cases.

Engineering work: Compared two ventilation arrangements through Python thermal models, 3D OpenFOAM solver runs and documented airflow visualisations.

23D OpenFOAM runs

OpenFOAMPythonPMV / PPDModel comparison
Numerical methods & validation plan

The study combines Python screening equations, an eight-hour thermal model and two 3D buoyant OpenFOAM cases. Source code, computed fields, solver logs and plots are available in the repository.

Validation stage: Current OpenFOAM cases cover an initial 600-second interval on a 3,840-cell mesh. Mesh refinement, longer-duration convergence and benchmark or measured-data comparison are planned next steps before design application.

Micro Tunnel Boring Machine · Bored Tunnelers

Helped advance a student-built micro tunnel boring machine through subsystem research, team CAD design, fabrication and prototype integration. The project earned the 2024 Not-a-Boring Competition Rookie Award.

My contribution: Led research coordination, organised mechanical subsystem investigations and design evidence, and supported technical communication across the Bored Tunnelers team.

CAD + buildDocumented visuals
2024Rookie Award · team
Micro-TBMCAD reviewMechanical researchTeam coordination
Engineering brief & development goals

Team CAD and outdoor prototype photographs document the mechanical design and assembly. Bored Tunnelers received the Rookie Award at the 2024 Not-a-Boring Competition.

Design objectives: The team brief specified modular construction, a target 100 kW power supply, IP68 water resistance, supervised automation, reduced mass, high excavation speed and front/lateral obstacle detection across clay, sand and hard-soil conditions. These are project requirements rather than independently verified operating results.

Hydraulic, electric, pneumatic and mixed system architectures were considered during design exploration.

METHOD NOTES

Research habits

Traceable methods, sound calculations and clear technical communication.

01

Separate model layers

Distinguish a fast screening equation from a spatial solver and a physical measurement.

02

Keep inputs traceable

Record geometry, material or flow assumptions, boundary conditions and solver settings.

03

Document engineering checks

Record conservation, sensitivity, mesh and cross-implementation checks alongside the findings.
