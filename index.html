02 / METHOD

Good work leaves a trail.

My project notes aim to make the path from question to conclusion inspectable.

01 — FRAME

State the question

Define the system, the decision the analysis should support and what is out of scope.

02 — MODEL

Write assumptions

Show inputs, equations, boundary conditions and the reason for each simplification.

03 — CHECK

Test the result

Use conservation, sensitivity checks, unit tests or comparison against a reference.

04 — REPORT

Mark the limits

Separate calculated evidence from concepts, visual sketches and unverified claims.

03 / PROFILE

About + technical toolkit

Mechanical engineering, with a continuing pull toward aerospace systems and useful computation.

I’m Sibly Noman, a mechanical engineering student in Kuopio, Finland.

My interests run from thermal management and fluid flow to satellite communications, antennas and low-power IoT. I enjoy research that asks a clear question, keeps its assumptions visible and communicates what the evidence can—and cannot—say.

I have worked in student engineering teams, led research activities and contributed to ESG reporting and carbon-footprint analysis. I’m currently completing a Bachelor of Engineering at Savonia University of Applied Sciences.

Alongside technical work, I bring project planning and cross-disciplinary coordination. I hold IPMA Level D certification and placed second with Team Savonia Synergy at Finland’s 2026 Project Management Championship.
