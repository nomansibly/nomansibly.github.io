Hydraulic Rotational Test Bench

A five-person Savonia RDI team developed and commissioned an instrumented teaching rig with an orbital hydraulic motor, pinion and flywheel ring gear, a tilting frame, safety guarding and Parker IQAN controls.

My role: Purchasing, supplier contact, meeting documentation and final report compilation; joined team commissioning and troubleshooting.

500–1,000RPM tested

1.5 mmsensor gap after fix

TECHNICAL NOTES + LIMITS

Initial proximity-sensor triggering at a 5 mm gap was unreliable; the team introduced adjustable brackets and confirmed reliable operation at 1.5 mm. Resonance around 900 RPM prompted frame gussets and base dampers. The report describes resolved issues during team testing.

Indoor Air Lab

A reproducible ventilation study combining Python finite-volume screening, an eight-hour thermal model and two executed 3-D buoyant OpenFOAM cases.

Verification status: Executed solver snapshots with logs and numerical checks; mesh independence and benchmark agreement not yet demonstrated.

3,840cells / 3-D runs
600 ssimulated time
2vent layouts
OpenFOAMPythonPMV / PPDVerification
TECHNICAL NOTES + LIMITS

The public browser lab recomputes the screening equations; it does not run OpenFOAM in the browser. The repository includes Python screening and transient models, two coarse startup URANS snapshots, computed fields, plots, solver logs and checks.

Limits: the 3-D cases are early 600-second snapshots. They are not steady, mesh-independent or experimentally validated design predictions. Further refinement and physical validation remain necessary.

METHOD NOTES

Research habits

Verification and limitations are part of the result.

01

Separate model layers

Distinguish a fast screening equation from a spatial solver and a physical measurement.

02

Keep inputs traceable

Record geometry, material or flow assumptions, boundary conditions and solver settings.

03

Report checks and limits

Show conservation, sensitivity, mesh or cross-implementation checks; state what remains unverified.
