METHOD NOTES

Research habits

Verification and limitations are part of the result.

01

Separate model layers

Distinguish a fast screening equation from a spatial solver and a physical measurement.

02

Keep inputs traceable

Record geometry, material or flow assumptions, boundary conditions and solver settings.

03

Report checks and limits

Show conservation, sensitivity, mesh or cross-implementation checks; state what remains unverified.
